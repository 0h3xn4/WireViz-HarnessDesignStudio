## Waivers
Fourteen waivers were granted by the owner, T-18 to T-31; T-32 (2026-10-12) is a fifteenth record of a related kind, a release decision taken on the owner's instruction, not a waiver in the owner's words. For T-18 (default branch not protected) and T-19 (packages not signed), both on 2026-10-08, the owner gave no reason. T-20 to T-30, on 2026-10-09, each record the one circumstance the owner stated (that no further feedback from people will come) and no other reason; they waive the actions that need a person (A-01, A-02, A-03, the remainder of A-04, A-07, the remainder of A-08, A-11, A-12, A-13, A-16) and the clean-VM test and usability steps of the release procedure. A waiver accepts that a requirement is not met; it does not meet it. T-31 (2026-10-09), in the owner's quoted words, waives the condition of step 14 of `docs/RELEASE.md` that the open decisions be settled before the version drops `rc`: the owner signed off 0.1.0rc8 and released 0.1.0 (`SIGNOFF.md`) with the open decisions still open. For 0.1.0 the real values and parts (D-10, D-11, D-12, D-15) were not waived and not done, and nothing in the project said otherwise. T-32 (2026-10-12), on the owner's instruction "Release as 1.0.0" and "Make default decisions so that I'm not needed", records version 1.0.0: D-10 and D-15 are recorded as the defaults the tool implements and D-11 and D-12 as values each project supplies (D-144, D-145); the assistant's defaults under the owner's delegation, which the owner can change, and no values were supplied. No other waiver requested or granted. A finding of the design rule check can be waived inside a project with a written reason (existing mechanism); that is a design waiver and not a waiver of the standards for the tool.

**Note of 2026-10-21: macOS was removed after 1.5.0 (D-168); the rows and the note below that name macOS describe what 1.2.0 to 1.5.0 shipped.** **Note of 2026-10-20 (versions 1.1.0, 1.2.0, 1.3.0 and 1.4.0).** No waiver was requested or granted for these releases, and no row was added; the rows above are the records of their dates and are not rewritten. (2) T-30 (no clean-machine installation test) names an Ubuntu VM; the release workflow tests the Windows and macOS packages on GitHub's machines (install, run, uninstall; mount, copy, run), but no person has installed any package on a clean machine without a network (as `SIGNOFF.md` words it), and the owner has not worded a waiver specific to Windows or macOS (`SIGNOFF.md` records T-30 as standing). (3) T-27 names "the Outline" as an accessible view; the Outline was replaced by the Project tree in 1.1.0, and the interface of 1.3.0 is new (D-150 to D-159) and 1.4.0 added the Edit wire tab and the device library buttons (D-160 to D-165). No person has used it in a usability session or with a screen reader; the automatic checks are `tests/test_ui_final.py` (accessible names, tooltips, the Tab walk, visible focus). The waivers T-18 to T-31 and the record T-32 stand as written. A waiver accepts that a requirement is not met; it does not meet it.
