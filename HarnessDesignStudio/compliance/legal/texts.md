# Legal texts: the one source

Date: 2026-11-09. Decision D-205 (brief rule 6, "one text, used everywhere").

Every legal text of Harness Design Studio is written here, once. Every other place copies it word for word: the READMEs, the app (About dialog and first-run notice), the generated outputs, the release notes and the website. A test (`tests/test_legal_texts.py`) fails when a copy differs from this file or when a place that must carry a text does not.

**How to change a text.** Change it here. Run `python -m tools.gen_legal_texts` (it writes `src/harness_design_studio/core/legal_texts.py`). Then change the copies listed in the test. Do not change a copy alone.

**Format.** Each text is the block quote under its heading. Keep it on one line of the block quote, with no line breaks inside it. Words in curly braces, such as `{tool}`, are filled in by the program.

**Not legal advice.** The texts were written by the owner's brief and drafted into this file by an AI (Claude, through Claude Code). `lawyer-questions.md` asks a lawyer whether they are enough.
