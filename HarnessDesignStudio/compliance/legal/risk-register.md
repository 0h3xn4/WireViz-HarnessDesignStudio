# Legal risk register

Date: 2026-11-09. Phase 1 wrote the risks; Phase 2 (the pull request) updated the Status column. Likelihood and impact are the Phase 1 ratings: they are not lowered here, because a measure that is not yet tested or decided does not remove the risk. Source: `legal-audit.md` in this folder. Not legal advice.

This register is separate from `compliance/RISK_REGISTER.md`, which holds the engineering risks of the tool. The IDs here start with `LR-`.

**Scale.** Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are low = 1, medium = 2, high = 3. Score = L × I. The list is sorted by score, then by ID. "Owner" says who acts: **me** = Claude (the AI that does the work), **you** = the project owner. Status values: `open` (nothing done yet), `decision needed` (waiting for the owner), `planned` (a Phase 2 step is named).
