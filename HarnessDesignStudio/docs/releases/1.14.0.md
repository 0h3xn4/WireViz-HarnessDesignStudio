This release gives you a quick way to make a block diagram, a Role button you can see, and a clearer diagram whose labels you can move. It came from workshop and owner feedback: the tool was too much for a systems engineer who only wants a block diagram, labels lay on top of each other, and things should be movable. The calculations, the 39 design rules and the numbers are as in 1.13.0.

### The four changes

- **Quick mode.** Turn it on in the Role button's menu, in the View menu, on the Settings page or with Ctrl+K. The window shows only Home, Model and Settings and an **Export diagram** button; you click two devices to connect them, with no pin window (Undo and Edit pairs are one click away), and **Add device** only asks for a name. It is off by default, works with every role, and the page *Make a block diagram in five minutes* (`docs/user-manual/quick-diagram.md`) shows the steps.
- **Export diagram.** **File > Export diagram** (or Ctrl+K) writes the block diagram as SVG, PDF and PNG, cut to its content. It needs no project folder, no harness and no checks.
- **Clear diagram, labels you can move.** Labels and connector boxes on a connection no longer overlap each other, the devices, the lane titles or the **More** button. Drag the pill of an interface, the label of a connection, the connector boxes at its ends or the vertical piece of its line; Undo works, and *Reset* in the right-click menu puts a label back.
- **Role button.** The top bar shows your role (for example *Role: Systems engineer*); a click lists the three roles, the same as **View > Role**.

### Upgrading

There is no migration: the file format keeps its schema version (1) and a project from 1.13.0 opens as it is. The only new project file is the optional `diagram/marks.json`, which holds where you moved labels to; it is written only when you move something and is not part of the model hash. The files the tool writes for the packaged examples are the same as in 1.13.0, apart from the tool version in the stamp and the model hash that follows from it.

### Known limits

- **Nobody looked at the program on a real display.** The window was tested with Qt's offscreen platform (texts, widgets, states and renderings), not by a person at a screen.
- **Small leftovers.** The marks of a deleted connection stay in `diagram/marks.json`, unused. Quick mode still shows the *Checking the design rules...* progress in the status bar and the *N pending* chips of the project tree. Only **Export diagram** cuts the sheet to its content; the normal export keeps its fixed sheet.
- **Lane boxes, connector rows and interface routes cannot be dragged**, on purpose: their order carries meaning.
- **No compliance and no sign-off is claimed.** The owner has not signed this release off. The packages are not signed (Windows SmartScreen shows *Windows protected your PC*: **More info**, then **Run anyway**), and the example values are placeholders.
