- **A twisted pair is counted once in the bill of materials and the mass.** A wire part can say how many conductors one metre of it carries (`conductors`; 2 for a twisted pair). Every wire that names the part is one conductor of it, so the BOM and the mass now count the metres of the cable, the sum of the wire lengths divided by that number. Before, a pair of two 3 m wires showed 6 m of cable and twice the mass. A part without the field counts as before. The parts import reads a *Conductors* column, and the Inspector and the part summary show the number. **In your own projects set `conductors` on your pair parts: the tool does not guess it.** The starter part `EX-WIRE-TWISTED-SHIELDED` has 2; a quad made from that part counts two pairs.
- **A waiver is tied to the finding it was made for.** A waiver records a short hash of the rule and the statement of its finding. If the finding says something else later (a number or a name in its text changed), the waiver no longer applies and the finding is open again; the DRC report lists it under *Waivers that no longer fit*, with the old reason. A waiver made before keeps applying as before.
- **The Problems panel says why a waived problem is open again.** The card names the earlier reason and says that the waiver no longer applies; the *Waiver* column of the Checks panel reads "Waiver no longer fits".
- **The documents no longer quote a model hash.** The transcripts show `<hash>`, because the value changed with every release.
- **Unchanged for existing work.** No design rule was added (still 39). A project that has no waiver and no pair part with `conductors` is written byte for byte as before, apart from the tool version in the stamp and the model hash that follows from it. Of the files the shipped examples write, only the BOM, the mass table and `export.json` differ from 1.10.0, apart from the version stamp and the model hash.
- **Not in this release / for you to know.** Still open: the owner's decisions on the standards and quality process, variants and a sharded `links.json`, and the soldering questions for the AIT engineers (`docs/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`). The drawing and the editor have not been used on a real display by a person. Nobody has signed this release off as the owner; this page is not a statement of compliance.
