# Design rules

Generated by `python -m tools.gen_rule_docs` from the rule registry; do not edit by hand.
Errors cannot be waived; warnings can be waived with a reason of at least 10 characters.
Rules that need numbers from your standards (see PLACEHOLDERS.md) check nothing until those are filled in, and the rule `unchecked-config` says so.

## Logical rules (instant, in the editor)

| Rule | Severity | What | Why | How to fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cross-strap` | warning | A nominal unit is connected to a redundant one. | One failure could affect both chains. | Connect to a redundant copy, or waive with a reason. |
| `no-connector` | info | An interface has no connector chosen on an end. | Without connectors it cannot become wires. | Choose connectors (automatic fix available). |
| `unconfirmed` | info | Connectors were chosen by the tool and not confirmed. | Nobody should assume auto-filled choices were reviewed. | Confirm them (fix available). |
| `isolated` | info | A unit has no interfaces. | It will not appear in any harness. | Connect it, or ignore. |

## Also reported as errors

Mismatches found by the independent verifier (`verify-mismatch`): a wire missing, extra, swapped or duplicated compared with the interfaces; a wire that starts and ends on the same pin (`wire_same_pin`) or joins two pins of the same end (`wire_same_end`); a wire label (`wire_signal_mismatch`) or a signal written on a cable connector pin (`cable_pin_mismatch`) that differs from the pinout, so two wires with exchanged pins are found. Fix: regenerate the harnesses.

The same rule name also reports a stale generation: the design changed after the harness plans were generated. Fix: generate again, then export again.

A harness whose interface type is shielded must have a shield group covering its wires (`shield_missing`). The verifier does not check the gauge, colour or part of a wire.
