# M8 demo note: closing open items (release candidate 0.1.0rc2)

You chose "close the open items". Here is what each item became.

**Done**
- **Drawing preview** (was: no preview in the editor): Harness plans tab, **Drawing** tab. It paints the same sheet description the exporter writes, with previous/next for multi-sheet harnesses, and updates when the design changes. Stamped "preview".
- **Routing sketch** (was: segments only listed): the first drawing sheet starts with connectors and branch points as boxes and segments with lengths. It is a schematic tree, not to scale, and is omitted above 14 nodes. It appears in SVG, PDF and the preview.
- **Outline tab** (was: canvas invisible to screen readers): every unit with its interfaces as a plain tree; selection follows the diagram both ways. This is the item-by-item accessible route; the canvas itself is still one announced object.
- **Arrange diagram** (was: no automatic layout): units stay in their zone lanes, ordered by the average position of their neighbours in other lanes, stacked without overlap; deterministic, one undo step, idempotent. Test: shuffled positions get shorter total link length; at stress size (200 units) it takes under 2 s.
- **Large UI scales** (was: small canvas at 150%+): the bottom panel is capped at 45% of the window and messages use the room available. A 150% screenshot at 1440x900 now gives the canvas about 250 px height (it was about 120).
- **Speed** (edits and undo at stress size): profiled. Undo's own logic is 44 ms; the rest of the 200 ms is the viewport repainting 2,000 links in software rendering, so I stopped there. Edits stay about 100 ms.
- **Bug found by the full-suite run and fixed**: a message timer firing after its window was closed raised an error in the Qt event loop and made an unrelated GUI test fail now and then (it failed three times in full runs). Fixed, with a regression test; two further full runs were clean. Also hardened the progress dialog for the background workers.
- Docs, guide, changelog (0.1.0rc2) and decisions D-116 to D-119 updated.

**Not done, and why**
- **Multi-drop interfaces** (more than two units, for example a bus): still skipped with a warning. They need an engineering rule only you can give: daisy chain or star, who owns stub lengths, which connector carries the through-wiring, where splices sit. I will not guess (D-116). Tell me the rule and I will build it.
- **Individual canvas items for screen readers**: custom accessibility interfaces for graphics items are fragile in PySide6 and I cannot test them without a screen reader here (D-118). The Outline is the substitute.
- **Diagram scaling with the UI scale**: zoom and Fit already scale the diagram; I did not couple them to the UI scale.
- Edits at stress size are still about 100 ms, borderline against the 100 ms target.

**Tested**: 548 tests pass (1 skipped: needs a non-root user), core coverage 96%. New tests: sketch layout, drawn sketch, skipped sketch, outputs verify with a sketch, arrange (lanes, overlap, idempotence, shorter links, undo, determinism, speed), preview, preview follows the design, pagination buttons, outline both ways, arrange action and undo, late-toast regression. The release checklist passed on every step except the one flaky test (fixed afterwards); version files, package and self-test are at 0.1.0rc2.

**Still needs people**: owner answers on D-10, D-11, D-15, D-20 and the multi-drop rule; usability sessions; a manual screen-reader pass; a release build on Ubuntu 22.04 with clean-machine tests.
